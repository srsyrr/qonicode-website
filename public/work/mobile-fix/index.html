Mobile fix

A typical Upwork job: “my store looks broken on phones”. On a copy of the Skinhale theme I added the kind of code a previous developer might leave behind, checked only on a laptop, then found the cause of each problem and fixed it with phone and tablet rules only. The broken state is simulated.

  • Practice project
  • Not client work
  • Horizon 4.3.0

Problem by problem

M-01 The homepage slides sideways

Homepage · trust bar

Problem

On a phone the page could be dragged left and right, and the first promise box (“Free shipping over $50”) sat off the left edge where no one could see it.

Cause and check

  • Cause: three boxes fixed at 320 px each in one centred row, 1,008 px wide
  • Fix: below 990 px the boxes wrap and shrink, one per row on phones, three on a tablet
  • Sideways scroll before: 297–324 px on phones, 120 px at 768
  • After: 0 px at 360, 390, 414 and 768
  • Desktop keeps the original rule

Just below the hero. On desktop (1440) the slider shows no change.

Files changed and how it was checked

Change log

  • assets/store-custom.cssPhone and tablet rules only; the desktop rule is untouched
  • sections/trust-bar.liquidThe boxes may wrap below 990 px

How I would run this for a real store

  1. Collaborator access with Themes permission only.
  2. Work on a copy of the live theme, so shoppers never see half-finished work.
  3. Hand over a preview link and a plain list of changes; the owner publishes.

Also tested

  • Page width compared with screen width on 3 pages × 5 widths
  • Desktop 1440: homepage, collection and product page compared pixel by pixel, 0 different outside the photos

M-02 Promo text box runs off the screen

Homepage · promo banner · new setting

Problem

The “Your new weekend ritual” box covered the photo and ran off the right edge of the phone, so part of the text and the button were hard to read or reach.

Cause and check

  • Cause: the box placed with desktop values: 72 px from the top and left, 440 px wide, 44 px heading
  • Fix: on phones the text sits below the photo, full width, smaller heading
  • No part of the box past the screen edge at 360, 390 and 414 px
  • Both options of the new setting previewed at 360 px

Bottom of the homepage on a 360 px phone.

Files changed and how to edit

Change log

  • sections/promo-banner.liquidPhone layout, plus the setting “Text on phones”

How to edit

  1. Customize → Home page → Promo banner → Text on phones.
  2. “Below the photo” (default) or “On the photo”: then the text sits at the bottom of the photo, inside the screen, on a taller 4:5 photo.

Also tested

  • “On the photo” option checked at 360 px

M-03 Header icons too small to tap

Every page · header

Problem

The menu, search, account and cart icons were tiny and clipped; the menu icon was barely visible and easy to miss with a thumb.

Cause and check

  • Cause: a “compact header on phones” rule shrank each icon to 24 × 24 px with no padding
  • Fix: the rule removed, icons back to the theme's size; the logo letter spacing slightly smaller so everything fits at 360 px
  • Before: menu 32 × 32, cart 24 × 24 px
  • After: menu 46 × 46, cart 44 × 44 px (target at least 44 × 44)
  • The menu opens and closes at 360 px

Top of the homepage on a 360 px phone.

Files changed and what else was checked

Change log

  • assets/store-custom.cssThe compact-header rule removed; logo spacing on phones

Measured on

  1. Homepage, Face care collection and a product page.
  2. 360, 390 and 414 px wide.

Also tested

  • Menu button and cart icon measured on every page and width, plus “is it on the screen?”

M-04 Add to cart thin and at the bottom edge

Product page · first screen

Problem

On a 360 × 800 phone the Add to cart button was partly below the first screen, and only 32 px tall with small text.

Cause and check

  • Cause: a “taller photos on phones” rule (3:4) and a “compact buttons” rule (32 px, 12 px text)
  • Fix: a square photo on phones and Add to cart at least 48 px tall with normal text
  • Before: 32 px tall, bottom edge at 804 px on an 800 px screen
  • After: 52 px tall, bottom edge at 704 px (360), 734 (390), 758 (414): always on the first screen
  • A product added to the cart at 360 px

The whole first screen of the product page on a 360 × 800 phone.

Files changed and what else was checked

Change log

  • assets/store-custom.cssPhoto ratio and button size on phones only

Measured on

  1. 360 × 800, 390 × 844 and 414 × 896 screens.

Also tested

  • Tablet (768) and desktop (1440) button unchanged: 52 px tall, on the first screen

M-05 A long product name spills over the price

Collection page · product card

Problem

On the Face care collection, an 88-character product name ran over its price.

Cause and check

  • Cause: every card title had a fixed 3-line height to line prices up on desktop; a 160 px phone card needs about 6 lines
  • Fix: on phones, titles show at most 3 lines and end with “…”; the full name stays on the product page
  • Short titles keep the same space, so prices still line up
  • Checked at 360, 390 and 414 px

“Overnight Hydrating Sleeping Mask…” on the Face care collection, 360 px.

Files changed

Change log

  • assets/store-custom.cssTitle line clamp on phones; desktop height rule untouched

Test data

  1. The long name was already in the store as an edge-case product from Demo 1.

Also tested

  • Price clear below the title at every phone width

M-06 Found while testing: cart off-screen on tablets

Every page · not one of the added problems

Problem

At 768 px the cart icon was outside the screen and could not be tapped. It was already in the theme with this store's menu.

Cause and check

  • Cause: one long menu label made the menu column as wide as its text and pushed the icons past the edge
  • My first check missed it: the page did not scroll sideways, the header just cut the icon off
  • Fix: between 750 and 989 px the menu may shrink and extra items move into the theme's “More” list
  • Cart right edge at 768, 900 and 989 px: 728, 860 and 949 px, always on screen

Tablet header at 768 px: look at the right edge.

What changed in my testing

Change log

  • assets/store-custom.cssTablet-only header rule, 750–989 px

Lesson

  1. A sideways-scroll check is not enough: an element can be cut off without scrolling.
  2. An “is it on the screen?” check was added to the test script.

Also tested

  • The same defect showed up again in the pre-handoff QA on the main Skinhale theme (D3-F001) and was fixed there too
