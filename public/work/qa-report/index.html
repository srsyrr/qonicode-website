Finding by finding

D3-F001 Cart button off-screen on tablets

Verified defect · High · 3/3 runs

What happened

At 768 px the cart button in the Skinhale header sat at 780–824 px, outside the screen, on every page. A very long menu label (deliberate test data) made the menu column as wide as its text.

Re-test after the fix

  • Cart visible at 768 and 1024 px
  • No sideways scroll on 9 pages × 5 widths
  • Home, Shop all, product and cart at 1440 px: 0 different pixels before / after
  • Theme Check: same 0 errors / 6 warnings as before

Skinhale header. At 768 px the cart icon at the right edge is missing before the fix; at 1024 px both look the same.

Steps, fix and evidence

Fix

  • Tablet-only header rule (750–989 px)7 lines: the menu column may shrink and extra items move into the theme's own “More” list
  • Separate theme branch + PRReleased as a new version after the current one, so the owner merges it

Steps to reproduce

  1. Open any page at 768 × 1024.
  2. Look at the right end of the header: search and account are there, the cart is not.
  3. At 1024 px it is visible again.

Also tested

  • Reproduced in Edge too
  • Severity High: the cart cannot be reached on an agreed screen size; practical importance Medium (only stores with a very long menu label)

C34 Halmere: other products unchanged

PASS · hero page C31–C34

Check

Only the plate set uses the new hero page. A product on Dawn's normal page must look exactly as it does on the untouched theme.

Expected

  • Other products: no hero sections, pixel-identical to untouched Dawn
  • Hero page: no sideways scroll at 360 to 1440 px, Chromium and Edge (C31)
  • FAQ opens with Enter, focus visible (C32)
  • Comparison table fits at 360 px (C33)

Halmere, Everyday Stoneware Mug: untouched Dawn and the changed theme. Drag the divider: nothing moves.

How it was compared

Method

  • Pixel comparison3 products × 2 widths, main product area: 0 different pixels
  • Section orderRead from the page: header, product, the three new sections, Dawn's sections, the sticky bar

Why it matters

  1. A new template must not change pages that do not use it.
  2. The one edit to a Dawn file (hiding empty tabs) is proven harmless this way.

Also tested

  • Related products hidden in these captures: Shopify loads them later and they were not stable
